Reminder terms
  • Your browser asks for notification permission the first time you save a task with a reminder time.
  • The reminder time must be in the future when you set it.
  • Each reminder shows once. Changing its time sets it again.
  • Completed or deleted tasks don't send reminders.
  • Reminders are checked every 30 seconds while the app is open in any tab (a background tab can be up to a minute late) and when you come back to it.
  • If a reminder was missed while the app was closed, it shows when you reopen the app, unless it is more than 24 hours old.
  • There is no push server, so the browser can't wake the app when it is fully closed.
